Ask the data: the golden questions

Each dot is one question from tests/golden_data_questions.csv, placed at the match of the best Query Cookbook recipe; its colour is what Ask the data did. Below the dashed line (0.25) no recipe runs; above it, the recipe also has to use most of the question's words and every column it names.

Ask the graph: the golden questions

Each dot is one question from tests/golden_graph_questions.csv, placed at the share of its words (apart from the names it found) that the facts use. Below the dashed line (75%) the facts are shown as closest, not as an answer.
