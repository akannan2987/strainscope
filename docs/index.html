StrainScope: interactive figures

Multi-omics, machine learning and a knowledge graph for beneficial microbes.

Every figure here opens in your browser and works offline once loaded. Hover for details, click the legends to hide a group, drag to zoom. The strains are simulated: the figures show the workflow working, not facts about real microbes. The guides that explain each one, and every step from a blank laptop to the finished app, are in the Handbook.
